Connect a calendar

Any app that publishes an .ics subscription works — dance, school, sports, your own calendar.

Where to find the URL
BAND — Calendar tab → Manage Events → Export Band Calendars → copy the address.
Google Calendar — Settings → the calendar → Secret address in iCal format.
Canvas — Calendar → Calendar Feed (bottom right).
Apple / iCloud — share the calendar publicly, then copy the link.
A webcal:// link is fine — it gets rewritten.
