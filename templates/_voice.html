{# AJ 2.0 — voice. Built on the browser's own SpeechRecognition and SpeechSynthesis rather than a speech API, for three reasons: it costs nothing, it adds no key to a personal app, and dictation never leaves the device on browsers that recognize locally. The trade is real and stated in the UI — support is good in Chrome, Edge, and Safari, and recognition is absent in Firefox. Everything degrades to typing. Three behaviors: * a mic button on any input marked [data-voice-input] * a speak button on any block marked [data-voice-read] * hands-free in the war room: talk, it sends, AJ answers out loud The toggle lives in localStorage, not the profile, because whether AJ talks is a fact about the device you're holding — the phone in the truck says yes, the laptop in a quiet office says no. #}
